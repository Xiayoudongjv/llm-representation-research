# Paper A reviewer attack matrix

This matrix records the release-facing response to predictable objections.
It does not add evidence or widen the scientific claim scope.

| ATTACK | SEVERITY | VALIDITY | CURRENT_DEFENSE | CLAIM_CHANGE_REQUIRED | MAIN_TEXT_RESPONSE | SUPPLEMENT_RESPONSE | UNRESOLVED_LIMITATION |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NOVELTY_TOO_CLOSE_TO_PROBING | high | valid concern | Position the contribution as a scoped decomposition of registered operational measurements; avoid an absolute novelty claim. | Use scoped contribution language. | Distinguish direct fixed-readout reuse from restricted FIT-only calibration. | Give the C1-C3 evidence map and prior-art positioning. | The literature boundary still requires ordinary related-work review. |
