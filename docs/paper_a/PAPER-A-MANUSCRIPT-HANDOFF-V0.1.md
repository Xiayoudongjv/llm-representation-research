## CENTRAL_RESEARCH_QUESTION

How do fixed-readout operational compatibility, cross-depth degradation, and
restricted FIT-only recoverability vary across depth and across the three
tested language models?

## THREE_CONTRIBUTIONS

1. Direct fixed-readout reuse is distinguished from recoverability under
   deliberately restricted FIT-only calibration.
2. The three tested models show partially dissociable operational profiles
   across degradation, source/target dominance, and restricted recovery.
3. Full directed matrices, split/condition heterogeneity, a registered
   negative predictor result, and exploratory directionality are characterized
   with explicit interpretation ceilings.
