## Priority Tiers

- `TIER 0 - EMPIRICAL CORE`: current experimental evidence.
- `TIER 1 - ACTIVE NEAR-TERM`: calibration / constrained transport.
- `TIER 2 - DEPENDENT THEORY`: operators, overlap/interference, structural scaling.
- `TIER 3 - SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE`: Harness, Meta-Harness, RAG, world-model integration.
- `TIER 4 - LONG-TERM APPLICATION`: embodied AI, structured belief, active perception.
- `TIER 5 - ANALOGY / INCUBATION`: Kakeya, cognitive folding, unverified terminology.

Priority means proximity to current evidence, not intellectual value.
