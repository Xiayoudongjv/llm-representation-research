# Research Value Assessment v0.7

## Core Value

The project is increasingly a methods-and-evidence-audit study rather than a
steering-performance claim. It records how apparently strong representation
steering evidence changes when construction coupling, independent
representation validation, behavioral intervention, and independent
measurement validation are introduced sequentially.

## What Remains Valuable

The record retains a bounded positive result: held-out target-directed
representation movement in EXP-018. It also retains clear negative findings:
relational-specific invariant interpretation was not supported, EXP-017 did
not establish task-specific behavioral control, and EXP-019's frozen evaluator
failed independent generalization.

## Claim Boundary

This is not state-of-the-art steering performance and does not demonstrate
reasoning improvement or semantic task conversion. Its value lies in preserving
the audit trail and treating failed validations as central methodological
evidence rather than as material to optimize away.

## Future Scientific Standard

Any new behavioral targetness measurement effort needs a separately
preregistered evaluator and a new untouched confirmatory set. Final-200 is now
available only for descriptive diagnostics and hypothesis generation.
