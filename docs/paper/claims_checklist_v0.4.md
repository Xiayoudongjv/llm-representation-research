# Claims Checklist v0.4

## Supported

- Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B achieved 0.750 on the current frozen 80-item hand-designed short-answer benchmark after conservative acceptable-answer correction.
- Analogy was the lowest-performing group in both the 24-item and 80-item evaluations.
- Small-sample group ranking was not stable.
- Raw answer scoring was materially sensitive to acceptable-answer coverage.

## Partially Supported

- Behavioral task differences correspond to representation geometry.
- Relational validity predicts behavior.

## Not Supported

- Representation metrics explain behavioral difficulty.
- Steering improves reasoning.
- Generation-time intervention works.
- The benchmark measures general reasoning ability.
- Results generalize beyond the current model and setup.
