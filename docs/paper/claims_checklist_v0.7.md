# Claims Checklist v0.7

## Allowed Claim

A frozen TF-IDF plus logistic-regression evaluator achieved perfect performance
on the procedural corpus but failed preregistered independent generalization
criteria on a separately frozen 200-example set.

## Forbidden Claims

- Steering improves reasoning.
- Representation transition causes behavioral transition.
- The evaluator provides robust semantic task classification.
- EXP-017 output targetness was validated.
- Procedural classifier accuracy demonstrates semantic robustness.

## Current Wording Ceiling

- EXP-018 supports held-out target-directed representation movement in the
  stated controlled design.
- EXP-018 does not support relational-specific invariant interpretation.
- EXP-017 does not support a stable task-specific behavioral effect.
- EXP-019 failed measurement validation for independent behavioral targetness.
- Representation-behavior linkage and output-level behavioral targetness remain
  unresolved rather than disproved universally.
