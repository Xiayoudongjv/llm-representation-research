## EXP-019 Closure

The frozen evaluator achieved perfect procedural development performance but
failed its one-shot independently frozen Final-200 evaluation: BA 0.4850,
macro F1 0.4580, with logic and causality recall at 0.2600. EXP-017 remains
locked for targetness analysis. Final-200 may support descriptive error
analysis and hypotheses, but cannot be reused as an independent confirmatory
test for a redesigned evaluator.

## Next Boundary

No evaluator rescue is authorized. Any future behavioral-targetness evaluator
must be preregistered and tested on a new untouched independent final set.
