# EXP-014 Gemma Steering and Relational Preservation Replication

## Motivation

EXP-014 tests whether Qwen's representation-level calibrated centroid steering
and RSM-preservation patterns are observable in a second model family.

## Model and Layer Selection

The model is `google/gemma-3-1b-it`, run from the local cache in float16 on
CUDA. Gemma hidden-state layer 26 was selected before this experiment from
EXP-013 because it maximized controlled separation and paraphrase retention.
This is model-specific layer selection, not a universal optimum.

## Data

The analysis reuses exactly EXP-003's 24 controlled prompts: six prompts in
each of logic, causality, analogy, and definition. Inputs are raw plain text;
no Gemma chat template is used.

## Steering Method

For every ordered source-to-target pair, the fixed raw centroid difference is
`delta = centroid_target - centroid_source`. Source-group representations are
changed only in memory as `h' = h + beta * delta`, for the predeclared betas
`[-1.0, -0.5, 0.0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0]`.

## Metrics

The experiment records source and target cosine similarity, target assignment,
relative perturbation norm, and source-group RSM Pearson correlation, IVS
(`1 - RSM Pearson`), and RSM Frobenius distance. RSM is a proxy for relational
preservation only.

## Results

The fixed local float16 CUDA run completed without an out-of-memory error or
numerical exception. All 12 ordered transitions reached target assignment at
least 0.5 and eventually reached 1.0.

- Mean target assignment was 0.556 at beta 0.5, 0.875 at beta 0.75, 0.972 at
  beta 1.0, and 1.0 at beta 1.5 and 2.0.
- The first beta reaching assignment at least 0.5 was 0.25 for one transition
  and 0.5 for the other eleven. Full assignment first occurred at beta 0.75
  for six transitions, beta 1.0 for four, and beta 1.5 for two.
- Centroid-difference norms ranged from 45.956 to 69.676. Mean relative
  perturbation rose from 0 at beta 0 to 0.415 at beta 0.75, 0.553 at beta 1.0,
  and 1.106 at beta 2.0.
- Mean IVS increased from 0 at beta 0 to 0.017970 at beta 0.75 and 0.058671 at
  beta 2.0. Corresponding mean RSM Pearson values decreased from 1.0 to
  0.982030 and 0.941329.

Under the predeclared exploratory rule (lowest mean IVS among beta values with
mean assignment at least 0.9), the selected Gemma point is beta 1.0.

## Cross-model Comparison

Existing Qwen EXP-006 metrics are read descriptively; Qwen is not rerun. The
comparison does not assume that beta 0.75 transfers between models. At beta
0.75, Qwen had mean assignment 1.0 and mean IVS 0.002850, whereas Gemma had
mean assignment 0.875 and mean IVS 0.017970. Gemma therefore does not reproduce
the same beta-0.75 operating point, although it reaches near-complete mean
assignment at beta 1.0.

## Replication Assessment

The run independently classifies calibrated and multi-pair transition success,
the perturbation trade-off, the relational-preservation trade-off, and whether
a Qwen-like beta-0.75 operating point is observed in Gemma. Calibrated
transition success, multi-pair transition success, the transition-versus-
perturbation trade-off, and the relational-preservation trade-off replicated.
The Qwen beta-0.75 frontier only partially replicated.

## Limitations

- Only two models.
- Model-specific layer selection.
- Twenty-four prompts and four task groups.
- Centroid steering only.
- Representation-level evaluation only; no generation-time intervention.
- RSM is a proxy, not logical invariance.

## Interpretation Rules

The experiment may report that transition and relational-preservation phenomena
replicated or failed to replicate, and that an observed beta operating point is
model-dependent. It cannot show that steering improves reasoning, that geometry
causes behavior, that beta 0.75 is universal, or that RSM is logical invariance.
