# EXP-010 Representation Validity vs Answer Difficulty

## Research Question

Do representation-level indicators correspond to answer-level task difficulty?

## Why This Experiment Is Needed

EXP-001 to EXP-008 are representation-level. EXP-009 and EXP-009B provide a
preliminary behavioral baseline and scoring audit. EXP-010 links the two at the
group level as an exploratory correlation analysis.

## Inputs

- `results/exp003/layer_metrics.csv`
- `results/exp003/group_metrics.csv`
- `results/exp005/pair_summary.csv`
- `results/exp006/invariant_pair_summary.csv`
- `results/exp007/frontier_summary.csv`
- `results/exp008/constrained_aggregate_summary.csv`
- `results/exp009/group_accuracy.csv`
- `results/exp009b/audit_summary.json`
- `results/exp009b/group_accuracy_comparison.csv`
- `results/exp009b/audited_answer_results.csv`

## Method

No new model runs are performed. EXP-010 merges answer accuracy with layer 16
within similarity and group-level incoming/outgoing transition and invariant
aggregates. Pearson correlations are computed across only four groups and are
explicitly treated as exploratory and underpowered.

## Metrics

- strict accuracy
- audited upper-bound accuracy
- layer 16 within similarity
- incoming and outgoing delta norm
- incoming and outgoing IVS
- incoming and outgoing RSM Pearson correlation

## Expected Outcomes

### Outcome A: Some alignment

Some representation metrics align with group-level accuracy. This would suggest
a possible weak link between representation structure and answer difficulty,
requiring larger-scale testing.

### Outcome B: No clear association

The current representation-level proxy may not explain answer behavior.

### Outcome C: Unstable or contradictory results

The group-level sample is too small and answer evaluation must be expanded.

## Limitations

- only four groups
- small answer set
- no causal inference
- no intervention
- scoring remains limited
- representation and behavior prompts are not identical in all cases
