# EXP-016 Full Layer-Strength Validity Study

## Motivation

EXP-016 expands the three-layer EXP-015 pilot to test how controlled encoding,
centroid steerability, and relationally conservative steerability vary across
sampled depth.

## Hypothesis

The layer that best encodes task-associated structure need not maximize
steering success or minimize relational disruption.

## Models

Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B and google/gemma-3-1b-it are loaded from local caches in
float16 on CUDA. Cross-model comparisons use normalized depth only.

## Data

The fixed input is the unchanged EXP-003 set of 24 raw plain-text prompts: six
prompts in each of logic, causality, analogy, and definition.

## Layer Grid

Qwen indices are 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, and 28, divided by 28. Gemma indices
are 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 23, and 26, divided by 26.

## Beta Grid

The predeclared positive betas are 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00, 1.25, and 1.50.
Each is evaluated across all 12 ordered group transitions.

## Encoding Metrics

Per layer, the study uses the existing separation, silhouette, paraphrase
retention, within-similarity, and between-similarity definitions.

## Control Metrics

For every layer-beta combination, it records mean target assignment,
target-minus-source similarity, relative perturbation, and counts of pairs
reaching assignment thresholds.

## Preservation Metrics

RSM Pearson and IVS (`1 - RSM Pearson`) are computed with the existing source
group RSM definition. RSM remains a relational-preservation proxy only.

## Layer-role Definitions

- **Encoding:** highest separation score, with retention and silhouette only as
  supporting tie-break metrics.
- **Control:** the layer reaching mean assignment at least 0.90 at the lowest
  beta; ties prefer higher assignment then lower perturbation.
- **Safe control:** lowest IVS among combinations with assignment at least
  0.90, then lower perturbation.
- **Efficient control:** exploratory maximum assignment divided by perturbation
  among threshold-reaching combinations.
- **Best validity:** exploratory maximum of assignment minus IVS minus 0.1
  times relative perturbation.

## Results

Both local float16 CUDA runs completed without out-of-memory or numerical
errors. All sampled layers, six fixed beta values, and 12 ordered transitions
were evaluated for each model.

### Encoding metrics

Qwen's maximum separation was 0.073015 at layer 16 (normalized depth 0.571),
with silhouette 0.182165 and paraphrase retention 0.036424. Gemma's maximum
separation was 0.093294 at layer 26 (depth 1.000), with silhouette 0.111792
and retention 0.071401. Gemma's highest sampled silhouette was instead
0.139718 at layer 16.

### Control and preservation surfaces

For Qwen, layers 8, 12, 16, 20, and 24 reached mean assignment at least 0.90
by beta 0.75; layers 4 and 28 first reached it at beta 1.0. At beta 0.75,
layer 16 had mean assignment 1.000 and mean IVS 0.002850. The lowest-IVS
threshold-eligible setting was layer 4 at beta 1.0: assignment 0.917, IVS
0.000838, and relative perturbation 0.285.

For Gemma, layers 16, 20, and 23 reached mean assignment at least 0.90 by beta
0.75; layers 8, 12, and 26 first reached it at beta 1.0; layer 4 did not reach
the threshold in the fixed grid. At beta 0.75, layer 16 had mean assignment
1.000, mean IVS 0.000202, and relative perturbation 0.104. Final layer 26 had
assignment 0.875 and IVS 0.017970 at the same beta.

### Pair-level robustness

The Qwen control setting (layer 16, beta 0.75) had assignment 1.0 for all 12
pairs. Its safe-control setting (layer 4, beta 1.0) had median assignment 1.0,
minimum 0.667, maximum 1.0, median IVS 0.000448, and maximum IVS 0.002182;
its lower-IVS outcome includes one weaker transition.

Gemma's control/safe-control setting (layer 16, beta 0.75) had assignment 1.0
for all 12 pairs, median IVS 0.000124, and maximum IVS 0.000673. Its safe
result is not driven by a small subset of successful pairs.

### Operational roles

| Model | Encoding | Control | Safe control | Efficient control | Best exploratory validity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-1.7B | L16 | L16, beta 0.75 | L4, beta 1.00 | L28, beta 1.00 | L28, beta 1.00 |
| Gemma-3-1B-IT | L26 | L16, beta 0.75 | L16, beta 0.75 | L12, beta 1.00 | L12, beta 1.00 |

Qwen's encoding equals control but not safe control. Gemma's encoding differs
from both control and safe control, while its control equals safe control.

## Cross-model Comparison

Role depths are compared only after normalizing by the model-specific final
hidden-state index. Pair-level statistics accompany every aggregate to show
whether an apparent safe result is driven by only a subset of transitions.
Encoding depths differ (Qwen 0.571; Gemma 1.000), control depths coincide
closely (0.571 and 0.615), and safe-control depths differ (0.143 and 0.615).
Final-layer geometry did not correspond to safe control in either model. The
mid-depth Gemma layers substantially outperformed its final layer on IVS at
comparable assignment; Qwen's final layer was efficient and validity-favorable,
but not its geometry or safe-control peak.

## Layer-role Separation Assessment

`supported`: operational roles differ in both models. In Qwen, the encoding and
lowest-threshold control layer coincide but the safest qualifying setting is an
early layer. In Gemma, the final encoding peak differs from the mid-depth
control and safe-control peak. These are operational properties of this sampled
grid, not causal layer functions.

## Gate Decision

`PROCEED_TO_GENERATION_INTERVENTION`: the complete fixed grid yielded
interpretable, model-specific control and safe-control selections in both
models, with no numerical failure. A future generation-time pilot must still
predeclare its layer choice, beta, controls, and behavioral evaluation; this
representation-level result does not establish behavioral benefit.

## Limitations

- Only two small LLMs and seven sampled layers per model.
- Centroid steering, 24 prompts, and four task groups only.
- Representation-level analysis; no generation behavior yet.
- RSM is a proxy, not an exhaustive invariant.

## Interpretation Rules

The study may report that tested operational layer roles differ and that
model-specific layer calibration is warranted. It cannot identify causal
functional modules, establish a universal layer hierarchy, or support a
biological or cognitive interpretation.
