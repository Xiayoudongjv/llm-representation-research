# EXP-013 Gemma Cross-Model Geometry Replication

## Motivation

The Qwen findings currently rely on one model family. Cross-model replication
is required before treating task-associated geometry as robust.

## Model

`google/gemma-3-1b-it` has approximately 1B parameters, 26 transformer blocks,
and hidden size 1152. It runs in float16 on CUDA with raw plain-text prompts.

## Data

The experiment reuses exactly the EXP-003 controlled 24-prompt dataset: four
groups with original-style and paraphrase variants.

## Method

One no-grad, batch-size-1 forward pass is used per prompt with
`output_hidden_states=True`; only last-token aggregate metrics are retained.

## Layer Selection

Gemma hidden state 0 is the embedding output and index 26 is final. Selected
indices are 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 23, and 26, compared by normalized depth rather
than nominal layer number.

## Metrics

Metrics match EXP-003: within/between similarity, separation, cosine
silhouette, paraphrase retention, PCA variance, and per-group within similarity.

## Results

The local float16 CUDA run completed without an out-of-memory error. All eight
selected layers had non-degenerate cross-prompt representations.

- The strongest separation was at final layer 26 (normalized depth 1.00):
  0.093294.
- The strongest silhouette was at layer 16 (normalized depth 0.62): 0.139718.
- The strongest paraphrase-retention score was at final layer 26: 0.071401.
- Positive separation and silhouette co-occurred at layers 8, 12, 16, 20, 23,
  and 26; positive paraphrase retention occurred at layers 12, 16, 20, 23,
  and 26.

The curves are non-monotonic: controlled geometry becomes positive after the
early layers, rises through the network, and the final-layer separation rises
sharply while the final-layer silhouette is below its mid-depth maximum.

## Cross-model Comparison

Qwen and Gemma curves are plotted at their actual normalized depths without
interpolation. Qwen's highest controlled separation was 0.073015 at layer 16
(of 28), while Gemma's was 0.093294 at layer 26 (of 26). Gemma's highest
silhouette was instead at layer 16, so nominal layer numbers are not treated as
cross-model equivalents.

## Replication Assessment

Using the fixed criteria, task-associated geometry and the paraphrase-controlled
signal both replicated. The non-monotonic layerwise profile also replicated.
The mid/mid-deep-peak criterion did not replicate because Gemma's strongest
separation occurred at the final layer, even though its strongest silhouette
was at normalized depth 0.62.

## Limitations

- Only two models.
- Both are small language models.
- Only 24 controlled prompts.
- Last-token representation only.
- Four hand-designed task groups.
- No behavioral result for Gemma yet.
- No steering replication yet.

## Interpretation Rules

The result may show that a Qwen geometry finding replicated, partially
replicated, or failed to replicate in Gemma. It cannot establish universal task
geometry, semantic modules, or behavioral prediction.

## Gate Decision

`PROCEED_TO_STEERING`: both task-associated geometry and the
paraphrase-controlled signal replicated under the fixed criteria. This is a
representation-level gate only; it does not establish behavioral improvement.
