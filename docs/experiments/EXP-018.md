# EXP-018 Independent Validation

## Motivation

EXP-018 tests whether the earlier centroid-steering findings survive an
independent fit/evaluation split. Research Audit v1 identified two risks:
source/target centroids and nearest-centroid evaluation had been constructed
from the same representations, and low IVS could reflect a generic common
translation rather than task-directed relational preservation.

## Audit Findings Addressed

The experiment separates the three fit examples per group from the three
evaluation examples per group. Fit-only centroids construct the steering
direction and a separate fit-only multinomial logistic-regression probe is the
primary transition evaluator. Matched-norm random and opposite-direction
vectors are evaluated on the same held-out source examples. IVS is judged only
relative to the matched-random control.

## Frozen Design

- Models/layers: Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B L16; google/gemma-3-1b-it L16; Gemma L26 as
  the predefined secondary contrast.
- Betas: 0.50, 0.75, 1.00.
- Directions: all 12 ordered pairs among logic, causality, analogy, and
  definition.
- Conditions: task-directed, deterministic matched-norm random, and exact
  opposite task direction.
- The fit-only probe uses the frozen StandardScaler and L2 multinomial `lbfgs`
  LogisticRegression configuration. Each split has 12 probe training examples
  (three per class); its training accuracy is diagnostic only.

## Independent Probe

The frozen probe was fit once per model/layer/split from the 12 fit examples
and reused without refitting for baseline, task, random, and opposite
representations. All pair summaries record 12 training examples and class
counts of three for each of the four groups.

## Matched-Random and Opposite Controls

Random directions were deterministically seeded once per model/layer/split/
ordered pair, L2-scaled to the task-delta norm, and reused across beta values.
Opposite directions were exactly `-delta_task`. These semantics are enforced by
the runner and its synthetic tests; no raw vector arrays were persisted.

## Relational-Preservation Results

The preregistered IVS advantage is `IVS_random - IVS_task`; positive favors
TASK. It was positive in only 78/216 conditions (36.1%), with mean +.037607
but median −.000048. The positive mean is not a general effect: Gemma L26
Split A had mean +.246872, whereas the other five model/layer/split cells were
near zero or negative.

| Model/layer | Split | mean IVS advantage vs random | positive conditions |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Qwen L16 | A | −.000144 | 10 / 36 |
| Qwen L16 | B | −.000809 | 22 / 36 |
| Gemma L16 | A | −.000059 | 16 / 36 |
| Gemma L16 | B | −.000295 | 9 / 36 |
| Gemma L26 | A | +.246872 | 13 / 36 |

TASK versus opposite was likewise not systematic: its IVS advantage was
positive in 118/216 conditions (54.6%), mean +.075170 and median +.000001.
Absolute RSM correlations were often high, but they are not treated as
evidence of task-specific preservation.

## Split Robustness

Transition directionality replicated in both wording directions. Within every
model/layer/split cell, TASK exceeded matched random and opposite in all 36
ordered-pair/beta conditions. The TASK-minus-random target-probability
differences ranged in cell means from +.807423 to +.868032; TASK-minus-opposite
cell means ranged from +.918088 to +.975961.

Relational preservation did not replicate across splits: Gemma L26 had a large
positive mean advantage in Split A but a negative mean advantage in Split B;
the remaining cells did not show a positive systematic advantage. Overall
`split_generalization = MIXED`: transition directionality is consistent, while
the relational comparison is not.

## Audit Resolution

1. **Did held-out centroid steering generalize?** Yes, on the fixed 24-prompt
   controlled design: held-out centroid movement was target-directed in every
   model/layer/split aggregate.
2. **Did the independent frozen probe support target-directed movement?** Yes.
   TASK target probability and target-minus-source probability increased in all
   216 frozen conditions.
3. **Did TASK outperform matched random steering?** Yes for the primary probe:
   216/216 TASK target-probability changes were larger (mean +.836584,
   median +.879981). This is descriptive small-sample consistency, not a
   significance test.
4. **Did opposite steering provide directional specificity?** Yes for the
   probe: TASK exceeded opposite in 216/216 conditions (mean +.947121,
   median +.985508).
5. **Did TASK preserve RSM better than matched-random translation?** No. TASK
   had lower IVS in only 78/216 conditions, with split/model dependence.
6. **Can historical centroid-transition claims be upgraded?** **Yes, narrowly.**
   The claim can move beyond `operational_only` to held-out,
   probe-supported representation-level target-directed movement on this fixed
   prompt design. It is not a behavioral, reasoning, or general task-conversion
   result.
7. **Can RSM/IVS claims be upgraded?** **No.** IVS remains a generic
   perturbation diagnostic; task-specific relational preservation was not
   validated.
8. **Do EXP-016 layer-role results gain independent support?** No. This study
   validates a predefined direction at selected layers, not functional layer
   roles or a new best layer.
9. **EXP-017 gate:** `READY_FOR_HOOK_DIAGNOSTIC`. This authorizes only the
   preregistered technical hook diagnostic, not a full generation-time
   behavioral experiment.

## Claim Status

- `transition_validation = PASSED`: all three frozen requirements were met on
  this controlled held-out design—held-out centroid movement, independent probe
  movement, and TASK superiority to matched random.
- `relational_validation = FAILED`: TASK did not systematically improve IVS
  versus equal-norm random translation.
- `split_generalization = MIXED`: target-directed transition evidence was
  consistent across splits; relational evidence was not.

## EXP-017 Gate Decision

`READY_FOR_HOOK_DIAGNOSTIC` only. The hook diagnostic can test whether the
frozen intervention is implemented at the preregistered location. It does not
authorize a full behavioral run, and EXP-018 does not establish behavioral
improvement or safe-control.

## Limitations

- There are only three fit and three evaluation examples per group per split.
- The controlled prompt set is small and hand-designed; Split A/B also exchange
  original-style and paraphrase wording.
- The linear probe is independent of steering centroids but is trained on only
  12 examples; its training accuracy is diagnostic, not generalization evidence.
- The centroid evaluator remains centroid-related and is secondary.
- RSMs for a held-out source group use three examples, hence only three
  off-diagonal similarities per comparison.
- No generation, behavioral scoring, raw hidden-state persistence, or model
  weights were produced by EXP-018.
