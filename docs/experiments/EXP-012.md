# EXP-012 Frozen Behavioral Link Reanalysis

## Motivation

EXP-010 used the preliminary 24-item behavioral baseline. EXP-011D later
established a larger 80-item provisional frozen benchmark, so the
representation-behavior analysis must be revisited.

## Research Question

Do representation-behavior associations remain similar under the frozen
behavioral benchmark?

## Inputs

EXP-011D group accuracy replaces EXP-009 behavior. Representation inputs match
EXP-010 where possible, including complete final-beta EXP-006 invariant metrics
instead of the known malformed pair-summary fields.

## Method

No model is rerun. This is an n=4 group-level analysis using Pearson, Spearman,
leave-one-group-out sensitivity, and explicit comparison with EXP-010.

## Results

Frozen group accuracy was causality 0.950, definition 0.850, logic 0.750, and
analogy 0.450. The strongest positive Pearson correlation was outgoing final
IVS (r=0.398; Spearman rho=0.600). The strongest negative was incoming
target-minus-source similarity (r=-0.906; rho=-0.800).

The largest old-versus-frozen change was incoming final IVS, from r=0.220 in
EXP-010 to r=-0.291 here; incoming final RSM Pearson changed correspondingly
from -0.220 to 0.291. These sign reversals show benchmark sensitivity at a
descriptive level. Several metrics have sign-consistent leave-one-group-out
values, but all such checks use n=3 and do not validate an association.

## Interpretation Rules

Allowed conclusions are limited to whether descriptive correlations changed or
remained directionally similar, and whether benchmark choice materially affects
them. The analysis does not show that a representation metric predicts reasoning
ability, that geometry causes behavior, that a relationship is significant, or
that one metric explains task difficulty.

## Limitations

- Only four groups.
- Hand-designed task categories.
- Representation and behavioral prompts are not identical.
- The frozen benchmark still has only 20 items per group.
- Correlations are descriptive only.

## Next Step

Prioritize second-model replication rather than further interpreting n=4
correlations.
