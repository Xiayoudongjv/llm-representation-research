# EXP-019: Independent Behavioral Targetness Evaluator

## Status

- `status = CLOSED_NEGATIVE_RESULT`
- `evaluator_development = EVALUATOR_DEV_MIXED`
- `independent_generalization = FAILED_INDEPENDENT_GENERALIZATION`
- `EXP017_targetness = LOCKED_UNRESOLVED`

## Design

EXP-019 preregistered an output-only behavioral task-identity evaluator: word
and character TF-IDF features plus multinomial logistic regression. The
evaluator was frozen before Final-200 evaluation. Its input was response text
only; identifiers, provenance, topics, sources, prompts, and length metadata
were excluded from features.

## Frozen Development Result

The evaluator obtained balanced accuracy and macro F1 of 1.0000 on the
procedural development test. The development record also documented lexical
and template-structure risk, so this result was not treated as independent
semantic validation.

## One-Shot Independent Final-200 Result

The separately constructed, human-reviewed Final-200 set was frozen and then
evaluated exactly once. Balanced accuracy was 0.4850, macro F1 was 0.4580,
and accuracy was 0.4850. Logic and causality recall were both 0.2600,
definition recall was 0.5800, and analogy recall was 0.8400. The preregistered
criteria required balanced accuracy at least 0.70, macro F1 at least 0.70, and
recall at least 0.60 for every class; they failed.

## Interpretation

Perfect procedural performance did not generalize. The large
procedural-to-independent performance drop is consistent with substantial
dependence on procedural lexical or template structure. The independent result
does not establish semantic task-identity classification. Better analogy
performance is descriptive, not evidence of a universal class hierarchy.

## Contamination Boundary

Final-200 has now been used for its one-shot evaluation. It may be used for
error analysis, descriptive diagnostics, and future hypothesis generation, but
must not be reused as an independent confirmatory test for any evaluator
trained or redesigned after viewing these results. Any evaluator v2 requires a
new untouched independent confirmatory test set.

## Consequence for EXP-017

Output-level targetness measurement for EXP-017 remains unresolved. EXP-017
targetness analysis remains locked. The one-shot test will not be rerun, and no
post-hoc evaluator rescue, hyperparameter tuning, or Final-200 modification is
authorized by this result.
