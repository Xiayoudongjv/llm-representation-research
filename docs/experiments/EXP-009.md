# EXP-009 Answer-level Reasoning Evaluation

## Research Question

Do the task groups used in representation-level probes produce answer-level
behavior that can be evaluated and compared?

## Why This Experiment Is Needed

EXP-001 to EXP-008 are representation-level. They do not show reasoning
improvement or generation-time intervention. EXP-009 creates a behavioral
baseline before intervention.

## Model

`Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B`

## Prompt Set

Twenty-four deterministic answer-level prompts across logic, causality,
analogy, and definition, with six prompts per group.

## Method

The model generates normal answers to concise instructions. No activation
steering or hidden-state intervention is applied. Generated answers are scored
with simple transparent rules: acceptable answer phrases use word-boundary
matching, while multiple-choice items use the expected option letter. Results
are summarized overall and by group.

## Metrics

- overall accuracy
- group accuracy

## Expected Outcomes

### Outcome A: High accuracy across groups

The model can solve the controlled tasks, making future intervention evaluation
feasible.

### Outcome B: Uneven group accuracy

Some groups may be behaviorally harder and should be treated carefully.

### Outcome C: Low accuracy overall

The prompt set or scoring rules need redesign before intervention experiments.

## Limitations

- small dataset
- simple rule-based scoring
- no intervention
- no causal link to representation metrics
- model output parsing may be brittle
