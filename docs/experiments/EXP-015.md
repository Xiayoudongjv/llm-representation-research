# EXP-015 Layer-Validity Pilot

## Motivation

EXP-015 separates three possible representation-level regions: an **encoding**
region with the strongest controlled geometry, a **control** region with the
strongest transition success, and a **safe-control** region that combines high
transition success with low RSM-proxy disruption.

## Hypothesis

The strongest representation geometry need not coincide with the best
intervention layer. The pilot tests, rather than assumes, whether
`l_encode == l_control == l_safe`.

## Models

The fixed models are Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B and google/gemma-3-1b-it, loaded from
local caches in float16 on CUDA. Qwen indices are 8, 16, and 28 (depth divided
by 28); Gemma indices are 8, 16, and 26 (depth divided by 26).

## Data

The analysis reuses exactly the 24 controlled EXP-003 prompts: six prompts in
each of logic, causality, analogy, and definition. Inputs are raw plain text;
no chat template is used.

## Layers and Betas

The layer and beta sets are fixed before execution. Steering is evaluated at
betas 0.50, 0.75, and 1.00 for all 12 ordered source-to-target transitions.

## Metrics

Encoding metrics retain the EXP-003/EXP-013 definitions: within and between
similarity, separation, cosine silhouette, and paraphrase retention. Control
metrics aggregate target assignment, target-minus-source similarity, and
relative perturbation. Preservation metrics are IVS and RSM Pearson, where RSM
is only a relational-preservation proxy.

## Predeclared Definitions

- **Encoding layer:** highest separation score; paraphrase retention and
  silhouette are supporting tie-break metrics.
- **Control layer:** at beta 0.75, highest mean target assignment; movement is
  the tie-break.
- **Safe-control layer:** among layer-beta combinations with mean assignment
  at least 0.80, lowest mean IVS. If none qualify, the fallback is highest
  assignment minus IVS.
- **Exploratory efficiency:** assignment divided by relative perturbation.
- **Exploratory validity score:** assignment minus IVS minus 0.1 times relative
  perturbation.

For the gate, a beta-0.75 layer effect is predeclared as material if the range
across the three layers is at least 0.20 in assignment or at least 0.01 in IVS.

## Results

Both local float16 CUDA runs completed without an out-of-memory error or a
numerical exception.

### Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B

- Layer 8 (depth 0.286): separation 0.053874, silhouette 0.140287, retention
  0.002542; beta-0.75 assignment 0.986, IVS 0.001881.
- Layer 16 (depth 0.571): separation 0.073015, silhouette 0.182165, retention
  0.036424; beta-0.75 assignment 1.000, IVS 0.002850.
- Layer 28 (depth 1.000): separation 0.021971, silhouette 0.091165, retention
  -0.009905; beta-0.75 assignment 0.875, IVS 0.000908.

The encoding and beta-0.75 control layers both selected layer 16. The
safe-control definition selected layer 28 at beta 0.75: it met the 0.80
assignment constraint with the lowest IVS (0.000908). Thus encoding equals
control but not safe control for these tested Qwen layers.

### google/gemma-3-1b-it

- Layer 8 (depth 0.308): separation 0.002023, silhouette 0.066520, retention
  -0.000315; beta-0.75 assignment 0.806, IVS 0.002667.
- Layer 16 (depth 0.615): separation 0.006060, silhouette 0.139718, retention
  0.004137; beta-0.75 assignment 1.000, IVS 0.000202.
- Layer 26 (depth 1.000): separation 0.093294, silhouette 0.111792, retention
  0.071401; beta-0.75 assignment 0.875, IVS 0.017970.

The encoding layer selected layer 26, whereas both beta-0.75 control and safe
control selected layer 16. Gemma's beta-0.75 assignment range across layers was
0.194, below the predeclared 0.20 threshold, but its IVS range was 0.017768,
above the predeclared 0.01 threshold.

## Cross-model Comparison

The pilot compares normalized depths and does not treat equal absolute indices
as equivalent layers. It reports descriptive geometry-versus-control plots only
and uses no inferential p-values. Qwen's geometry-best layer also had the
highest beta-0.75 assignment among its tested layers, but its lowest-IVS
eligible setting was the final layer. Gemma showed a stronger separation of
roles: the final layer was geometry-best, while the mid-depth layer 16 was both
control-best and safe-control-best. Across these three-layer pilots, geometry
strength did not consistently predict lower IVS or the same control location.

## Gate Decision

`EXPAND_LAYER_VALIDITY_STUDY`: the tested roles differ in both models, and
Gemma's predeclared beta-0.75 IVS range across layers (0.017768) is material.
The result does not justify changing the fixed pilot settings after the fact;
any broader layer sweep is a separate experiment. A diagnostic gate is reserved
for numerical or methodological failure.

## Limitations

- Only three layers per model and three beta values.
- Centroid steering and four task groups only.
- Representation-level evaluation; no generation-time behavior.
- RSM is a proxy rather than an exhaustive invariant.

## Interpretation Rules

The experiment may report that the tested layers differ or match in
representation/control properties. It cannot establish a universal functional
hierarchy, identify a causal layer role, or support biological or cognitive
interpretation.
